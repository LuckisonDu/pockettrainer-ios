Learn how small machine-learning models work by training and testing them on your iPhone. PocketTrainer is a free student project by Luckison Du.

## Learn by doing

- Start with the bundled photo classifier and watch its learning curve.
- Try handwritten digits, clothing, flower and other table datasets, your own photos, or a compatible CSV.
- Change model settings, explore held-out predictions, and draw a digit for a trained digit model to recognize.
- Keep project files, training records and notes together. Export supported models for further learning.
- Read offline learning tips and the in-app Privacy & data page without an account.

## What needs a connection?

| Feature | Connection requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Bundled photo training and testing | Runs on the iPhone; no sign-in or download needed |
| Prepared datasets, saved models, files and notes | Work locally |
| Public dataset or project search and first downloads | Internet required |
| Optional connected tutor | Internet, user authorization and an eligible ChatGPT account with available plan usage; availability depends on the account and service |
| Optional image generation | Runs locally after importing a compatible Core ML model pack; a public download pack is not supplied in this version |

Core projects do not connect to the developer's computer. The optional tutor connects directly from iPhone to OpenAI. Before authorization, the app explains which questions, chat history and selected project details will be shared.

PocketTrainer is for small educational experiments, rather than medical, safety-critical or production decisions. Device storage, model compatibility and third-party service requirements apply to optional features.
